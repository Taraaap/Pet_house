About STAR Pet House

STAR Pet House is a trusted destination dedicated to the care, comfort, and well-being of pets. We provide a professional and nurturing environment where animals receive the highest standard of attention and care.

Our services include professional grooming, veterinary consultation, secure boarding facilities, and structured training programs. Each service is designed to ensure the health, safety, and happiness of every pet in our care.

In addition, we offer a curated selection of premium pet food, accessories, toys, and wellness products—ensuring quality, safety, and reliability in every item we provide.

Located in Bharatpur, Chitwan, we are committed to serving our community with integrity, compassion, and professionalism. Our goal is to create a trusted space where pets are treated with the care they truly deserve.
